Equipra — Problem Statement & Solution Architecture
Architecture & Design
1 Problem Statement: The Laboratory Logistics Crisis
⚠️ Ghost Inventory & Component Hoarding

Valuable development boards (TI mmWave Radars, Artix-7 FPGAs, SDRs) sit locked in departmental silos. Students cannot discover hardware owned by other departments, leading to duplicate procurement costs and stranded assets.

📝 Paper Registers & Damage Disputes

Physical register signatures lack verification. When an expensive sensor is returned defective, disputes arise over whether damage occurred prior to handover or during usage, creating friction and lab distrust.

⏳ Cumbersome Manual Approvals

Securing lab equipment requires chasing professors and lab attendants across campus for physical signatures, delaying student projects and critical hackathon sprint timelines by days or weeks.

📉 Zero Health & Runtime Visibility

Traditional inventory lists track only quantity on a shelf. Attendants have no real-time telemetry on operating temperature, device runtime, or vibration anomalies experienced during student testing.

Equipra — CodeHack 2026 Innovations & Key Features
Innovation Highlights
4 Specialized Innovations for CodeHack India 2026
🌐 1. Cross-Lab Collaboration Bundles & Shared Spaces Hardware Ecosystem
Traditional systems treat equipment as individual components. Equipra introduces Pre-Configured Hardware Bundles (e.g., Autonomous Rover Kit, mmWave Radar Vision Suite, Industrial IoT Mesh) and Shared Workbench Reservations. Multidisciplinary student teams can request cohesive hardware stacks alongside reserved lab benches with a single click.
🔍 2. AI-Powered Visual Fault Scan & 24-Hour Quarantine Automated Quality
Integrated computer vision compares photographic evidence captured at issue against condition at return. Any physical pin bending, scorched ICs, or connector damage flagged during return triggers an Automated 24-Hour Quarantine isolation cycle, preventing defective hardware from re-entering circulation.
📡 3. Live IoT Telemetry & Vibrometer Streaming WebSocket Ingest
Equipra includes a live telemetry ingestion pipeline. Microcontroller-equipped hardware projects stream real-time sensor metrics (temperature, vibration indices, battery voltage, uptime) via WebSockets directly into dynamic gauge and chart widgets on the student and faculty dashboards.
🛡️ 4. Student Hardware Trust Score (0–100) Algorithmic Accountability
A dynamic credibility algorithm rewards students for timely returns, flawless equipment care, and verified project completion. High trust scores unlock extended borrowing periods and priority access to high-demand equipment, solving the tragedy of the commons in shared labs.
🎟️ 5. Dynamic QR Digital Collection Pass Instant Handover
Once approved by both faculty and lab admin, students receive an encrypted, high-resolution Digital Collection Pass. Attendants scan the pass at the lab counter to instantly verify identity, check out the assigned hardware, and trigger an automated audit log with timestamp.
⚖️ 6. 1-Click Judge Quick-Access Bar & Demo Switcher Hackathon UX
Designed specifically for CodeHack 2026 evaluators, a non-intrusive floating switcher lets judges instantly toggle between Student, Faculty, and Admin personas without typing credentials, allowing complete evaluation in under 2 minutes.
